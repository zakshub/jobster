# Negotiation Intelligence

## Goal

Jobster should reason about negotiation as a situation, not as a fixed salary formula.

## Inputs

1. Who initiated contact.
2. Stage reached.
3. Hiring manager signals.
4. Recruiter urgency.
5. Number of interview rounds.
6. Role criticality.
7. Published salary range.
8. Internal salary clues.
9. Competing opportunities.
10. Current employment situation.
11. Notice period.
12. Remote flexibility.
13. Contract type.
14. Location and currency.
15. Benefits.
16. Candidate target.
17. Candidate minimum.
18. Negotiation anchor.
19. Walk away conditions.
20. Non salary priorities.

## Leverage model

Possible levels:

weak
limited
balanced
strong
very_strong

Leverage must be evidence linked.

Do not infer strong leverage merely from positive recruiter language.

## Strategy variables

Jobster can choose among tactics such as:

1. Delay compensation disclosure when useful.
2. Ask for the approved range.
3. Anchor against role scope and value.
4. Counter on base compensation.
5. Trade base against bonus.
6. Trade base against review timing.
7. Trade base against leave.
8. Trade base against equipment.
9. Trade base against contract security.
10. Trade base against working hours.
11. Trade base against title or scope where strategically useful.
12. Use silence rather than unnecessary concessions.
13. Preserve optionality while other processes continue.
14. Avoid revealing weak fallback positions.

## Output

Every negotiation analysis provides:

1. Situation summary.
2. Evidence.
3. Leverage level.
4. Employer likely constraints.
5. Candidate priorities.
6. Recommended strategy.
7. Opening position.
8. Acceptable movement.
9. Non salary alternatives.
10. Walk away triggers.
11. Draft message options.
12. Confidence.
13. Unknowns.

## Authority

Analysis can be autonomous.

Drafting can be autonomous.

External salary counters remain human approved until Zak explicitly delegates a bounded authority policy.

Offer acceptance always requires Zak.
