# Jobster Autonomy Policy

## Principle

Autonomy is permission bounded, not unrestricted.

Jobster can act automatically when the action is reversible, based on verified information, and covered by explicit policy.

## Fully automatic

1. Discover jobs.
2. Normalize jobs.
3. Deduplicate jobs.
4. Read descriptions.
5. Evaluate fit.
6. Reject obvious bad matches.
7. Shortlist strong matches.
8. Generate internal reasoning.
9. Prepare application materials.
10. Fill known factual fields.
11. Track statuses.

## Automatic when all required facts are verified

1. Submit supported ATS applications.
2. Use approved resume variants.
3. Use approved standard application answers.

## Stop and request Zak input

1. Unknown work authorization facts.
2. Sponsorship questions not already settled.
3. Salary history.
4. Legal declarations.
5. Disability or health disclosures.
6. Security clearance claims.
7. Criminal history questions.
8. Relocation commitments.
9. Questions requiring unsupported career claims.
10. CAPTCHA.
11. Unsupported form behavior.
12. Material ambiguity in compensation.
13. Final offer acceptance.
14. Contract acceptance.

## Negotiation

Jobster may independently analyze and recommend.

It may draft counters and negotiation messages.

External sending remains approval required until Zak explicitly delegates a narrower negotiation authority range.

Offer acceptance always requires Zak.

## Truth constraint

Jobster must prefer leaving a question unresolved over inventing a useful looking answer.

## Audit constraint

Every consequential automated action records:

1. What action happened.
2. Why it happened.
3. Which facts were used.
4. Which policy authorized it.
5. Which document or answer was submitted.
6. Timestamp.
7. Result.
