# Jobster Personal MVP Scope

## Goal

Prove one complete useful loop for Zak.

Discover
then understand
then decide
then prepare
then apply
then track
then learn

## Included in MVP

### CareerBrain

1. Structured career profile.
2. Capability map.
3. Preference map.
4. Role flexibility map.
5. Career direction map.
6. Compensation policy.
7. Evidence linked judgments.
8. Correctable decision records.

### Discovery

Initial source families:

1. Public remote job feeds.
2. Company career pages.
3. Direct ATS listings.
4. Google based job discovery through a replaceable search provider.
5. LinkedIn job alert intake.
6. User provided LinkedIn URLs.
7. Manual job URL intake.

LinkedIn is one source, not the system Jobster depends on.

### Evaluation

Every normalized job receives:

1. Eligibility result.
2. Role understanding.
3. Capability alignment.
4. Learnable gaps.
5. Hard blockers.
6. Interest estimate.
7. Career value.
8. Compensation confidence.
9. Pursuit priority.
10. Recommended positioning.
11. Explanation.
12. Confidence.

### Application

Initial ATS targets:

1. Greenhouse.
2. Lever.
3. Ashby.
4. Workday after the first three are reliable.

Jobster can:

1. Prepare a role specific resume variant.
2. Prepare useful cover letter content when appropriate.
3. Fill known factual fields.
4. Answer from an approved answer bank.
5. Upload approved documents.
6. Submit when policy allows.
7. Capture submission evidence.
8. Stop when required information is unknown or sensitive.

### Tracking

Application states:

discovered
normalized
evaluated
rejected
shortlisted
preparing
blocked
submitted
failed
recruiter_contacted
interviewing
offer
negotiating
closed

### Recruiter workflow

MVP behavior:

1. Classify incoming recruiter communication.
2. Summarize context.
3. Identify requested action.
4. Draft reply.
5. Assess leverage signals.
6. Recommend next move.

Automatic recruiter messaging is not required for the first MVP.

### Negotiation workflow

MVP behavior:

1. Build offer context.
2. Estimate leverage.
3. Compare offer with minimum, target, and anchor.
4. Identify non salary trade variables.
5. Produce strategy.
6. Draft counter options.
7. Require human approval before external commitment.

## Explicit non goals

1. Mass application spam.
2. CAPTCHA bypass.
3. Stealth automation intended to defeat platform controls.
4. Invented credentials.
5. Invented work history.
6. Autonomous offer acceptance.
7. Premature SaaS infrastructure.
